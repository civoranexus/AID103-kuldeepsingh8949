About CropGuard AI

CropGuard AI is an advanced plant disease detection system powered by deep learning and computer vision. Our platform helps farmers and agricultural professionals quickly identify plant diseases by analyzing leaf images with state-of-the-art convolutional neural networks (CNNs).

The system is trained on a comprehensive dataset of plant diseases and can identify various crop diseases with high accuracy, providing instant diagnosis and confidence scores to help users make informed decisions about crop management.

How It Works

1

Image Upload

Users upload a clear image of a plant leaf through our web interface. The system accepts common image formats (JPG, PNG, JPEG).

2

Image Preprocessing

The uploaded image is preprocessed: resized to 224x224 pixels, converted to RGB format, and normalized for optimal model input.

3

Deep Learning Analysis

Our trained CNN model analyzes the preprocessed image, extracting features and patterns to identify potential diseases.

4

Prediction & Results

The model generates predictions with confidence scores, showing the top 3 most likely diseases and their probabilities.
